WEBER 1891: SOURCE AUDIT AND HISTORICAL ASSESSMENT
22 October 2026. Research note prepared with Codex.

This is a source and mathematical audit performed in the drafting session,
not an independent audit verdict and not formal verification.

Historical conclusion
---------------------
1891 is a defensible upper-bound proposal under the stated rule allowing
new problem-specific deductions and repairs using an already published
organizing method. It is not a proved earliest year, a claim that the proof
was published in 1891, or an assessment of the probability of discovery.

The actual theorem is Heegner's (1952); the repair of the critical modular
step is due to Stark (1969). Pratt's 22 October 2026 comment points out that
the relevant material occurs in Weber's 1891 book, rather than only the
later Lehrbuch volume cited by Stark:
https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/20/an-experimental-ai-math-challenge-using-tenth-discriminant-bounds-to-explore-arguments-ruling-out-siegel-zeroes/comment-page-1/#comment-698625

The strongest source evidence for the METHOD, as opposed to statements
or elementary prerequisites, is Weber section 76, pp. 267-269. He explicitly
describes constructing transformation equations from functions permuted by
z -> z+2 and z -> -1/z, with coefficients rational in f(z)^24. This is the
product construction used by Stark. Section 95, p. 368 already compares
coefficients and factors the equations for known singular values through
degrees 24, 12, 6, and 3. Heegner's missing observation makes those
restrictions exhaustive rather than merely calculating known examples.

Resolution of the modular-equation gap
-------------------------------------
The previous inability to retrieve Stark's full paper has been resolved.
The original and the repository OCR were obtained from Michigan's current
public repository API. The full 12-page article was read; the critical
formula pages 23-25 were also rendered and inspected. The earlier local research note recorded the access limitation; it is
no longer the current state of the investigation.

Stark's gap is precise: Weber's Schlafli relation is not shown to have ONLY
the required transformed values as roots. A polynomial relation with extra
roots would invalidate the gcd step. Stark replaces it by an explicit
product, proves the coefficient functions are polynomials in f^24, proves
rationality through cyclotomic automorphisms, and substitutes back without
adding nonzero roots. This is not an appeal to modern class field theory.

The new paper specializes that construction to a prime q:

  F=f^3, U=f^24;
  M_infinity(z)=qz, M_nu(z)=(z+16nu)/q, 0<=nu<q;
  product_M [X-F(Mz)^e F(z)^(23qe)] = Sigma_{q,e}(X,U(z)), e=1,8;
  R_{q,e}(X)=Sigma_{q,e}(X^(23q+1),X^(8/e)).

It proves the transformations under S and T^2 explicitly, rather than
leaving the multiplier permutation implicit. The specialized root test is
an equivalence. The two gcd calculations isolate f^24 and f^6; Weber's
gamma_2 invariant then gives f^8 and hence f^2.

The exact multiplier in modern matrix notation is

  F((az+b)/(cz+d))/F(z)
    = exp(-pi i [db+ca+2d^2-2dc-2da]/8)

for the theta subgroup. This was derived from Weber's scanned p. 107,
including his different matrix convention, and checked on all 1024
admissible matrix residue classes modulo 16. The root selection at
theta+2j, j=0,...,7, gives the multiplier (-i)^j and hence exactly j=0,4.

Historical inputs and their precise places
------------------------------------------
H1: Gauss, Disquisitiones arithmeticae (1801), Section V, Articles
153-159 (forms and equivalence), 171-175 (reduction). The paper supplies
its small-prime form obstruction and finite reduction directly.
https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-4025

H2: Euler, Theorematum quorundam arithmeticorum demonstrationes,
Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae 10, 125-146, Theorem 10.
Written 1738, published 1747; publication is the cutoff date used.
https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/euler-works/98/
Original scan successfully downloaded from:
https://math.dartmouth.edu/~euler/docs/originals/E098.pdf
German translation successfully downloaded from:
https://download.uni-mainz.de/mathematik/Algebraische%20Geometrie/Euler-Kreis%20Mainz/98.pdf

Euler's original argument is brief about the strict decrease. The new
appendix gives that decrease, sign choices avoiding the extra mod-3 case,
and a proof that the trivial pair cannot be reached from a nontrivial one.
Conrad's modern exposition is credited as guidance, not treated as an old
source. The argument uses Euler's factorization and rational substitutions.
https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/ugradnumthy/descentbyeuler.pdf

H3: Heinrich Weber, Elliptische Functionen und algebraische Zahlen,
Braunschweig: Friedrich Vieweg und Sohn, 1891. All locations below refer
to this edition and printed pages, not scan-page numbers:
https://archive.org/details/ellipfuncundalg00weberich
https://archive.org/download/ellipfuncundalg00weberich/ellipfuncundalg00weberich.pdf

* Section 29: product identities for f,f_1,f_2.
* Section 35, pp. 105-107: transformation laws; the last formula on p. 107
  gives the cubed multiplier after converting matrix conventions.
* Section 49, pp. 147-150: modular function fields, theta subgroup,
  gamma_2, and f^8,-f_1^8,-f_2^8 as roots of X^3-gamma_2 X-16.
* Section 72, pp. 247-255: invariant modular polynomials, monicity,
  integer coefficients. Section 89, p. 338 explicitly uses degree two.
* Sections 73 and 76, pp. 255-269: products over transforms and symmetric
  coefficient functions. This is the historical METHOD supporting the
  specialized repair, rather than just old ingredients for a later method.
* Sections 87-88, pp. 331-337: singular j integrality and class polynomial
  of degree h. Section 88 uses induction and gcds. General irreducibility
  of class polynomials is NOT used.
* Section 92, pp. 350-352: gamma_2(z) in Q(j(z)) when 3 divides the middle
  coefficient B and does not divide A or the discriminant. Applied both
  at theta^2+q=0 and at tau^2-3tau+(q+9)/4=0.
* Section 94, pp. 360-362: f^8 and f^6 invariants. The statement combining
  them to f^2 is explicit on p. 361; Theorem II is on p. 362. The original
  proof's root-control issue is repaired in the new paper, not ignored.
* Section 94, p. 365: stronger f^3 assertion is explicitly conjectural and
  is NOT used anywhere in the proof.
* Section 95, pp. 367-368: phase-normalized product identity and factorizations
  for the known examples. The new proof also checks the identity directly
  by separating even and odd product indices.

Scope of the proof
------------------
The classical modular toolkit is stated and sourced as imported 1891
mathematics. The paper does not reconstruct all of complex analysis,
elliptic functions, or Weber's class-polynomial and gamma_2 theory. It is
self-contained beyond that explicit toolkit: it supplies the exceptional
modular repair, all class-number-specific deductions, the infinite descent,
and finite enumeration. This scope is stated in the abstract and introduction.

The later general theorem [Q(j):Q]=h is avoided completely: the cubic for
alpha^4 is strictly increasing, and its unique real root lies in (0,1),
so it has no rational root. This establishes the degree exactly where needed.
No modern algorithm for integral points, QRH, Siegel-zero result,
class-field existence theorem, or Shimura reciprocity theorem is used.

The two Euclidean-ring factorizations in the Diophantine finish are proved
by rounding coefficients, then using the Euclidean algorithm and Bezout.
They are applications of an existing elementary factorization method to
two explicit rings; no general theorem about ideal classes is invoked.
