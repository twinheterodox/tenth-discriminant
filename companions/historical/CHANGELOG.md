# Historical companion changelog

## historical-v0.1.0 - 21 October 2026

First publication of *No tenth discriminant: a modern reconstruction
using tools documented by 1830*.

- Gives the cutoff `q < 32,768` and a complete printed certificate leaving
  the nine negative fundamental discriminants of class number one.
- Adapts the elementary companion using explicit quadratic forms,
  quadratic congruence arithmetic, and determinant permutation expansion.
- Documents the historical foundations and states the interpretation of
  "results and concepts" underlying the proposed 1830 cutoff.
- Separates historical references from modern attribution, with credits
  beside the corresponding arguments and all needed proofs in the paper.
- Includes exact constant checks, 119 modular witnesses, a separate sieve
  of 4,095 candidates, and reduced-form enumeration of the nine survivors.
- Records the mathematical argument and historical eligibility as
  requiring separate review; no independent audit verdict is asserted.

This is a separate companion series. Its year is a proposed historical
cutoff, while `q` denotes the absolute discriminant.
