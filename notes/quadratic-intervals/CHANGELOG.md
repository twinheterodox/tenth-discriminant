# Quadratic-interval note: version history

This series treats general quadratic-field estimates arising from the
determinant method. It is separate from the numbered tenth-discriminant
cutoffs and the elementary and historical companions.

## quadratic-intervals-v0.1.0 — 26 October 2026

- First publication of *A uniform interval bound for inert-prime mass*.
- Proves the explicit interval estimate for every primitive nonprincipal
  real character, uniformly in both interval endpoints, with conductor
  allowance `(1/4) log(q) + log(log(q)+33) + 18`.
- Gives the logarithmic-derivative and split-prime corollaries, the
  separately conditioned residue consequence, and the precisely stated
  continuous-model limitation.
- Incorporates the two presentation clarifications requested by the
  reported model cross-check; the review record identifies its scope.
- Includes the eleven-page paper, LaTeX source, exact verification program,
  recorded output, model report, reproduction instructions, and checksums.
